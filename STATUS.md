- **Repknutar: pluggade hål** (2026-10-18, the user found the mode too easy). Some holes are plugged 🪵: no rope end can go there, and a rope lying over a plug counts as tangled (the plug is drawn on top with a red ring). Plugs start at 2 and grow to about half of the free holes (`repFor().pluggar`), always leaving 3 free holes. They are placed on the solved layout before the backwards scramble, so every knot is still solvable within its ⭐ moves (tested for knots 1–16 × 25 seeds). Scrambles are also longer (`steg = reps + l`). Measured over 40 knots each, the greedy hint now solves 40/40 at knot 3 but 33/40 at knot 14, so big knots need planning.
- **Trasselvinden: Repknutar** (2026-10-18, asked for by the user: "the other version" of the rope game). A second mode next to Pinnknutar, picked in the lobby. Coloured ropes with their ends in holes lie on top of each other; drag an end to an empty hole (or tap the end, then the hole). Only a rope with nothing on top can be lifted (🔒 + an explanation otherwise), and a moved rope lands on top, so the order matters. 2 ropes at knot 1 up to 9 from knot 14. Every knot is built backwards from a solved board with exact inverses of real moves, so it is always solvable within its ⭐ move count; solving within it gives bonus blad. The spider makes one such backwards step (it pulls the top rope and shoves it under). Same monsters, gear and hints (the hint makes the best move). Own record `HeroState.trassel.rep`, title **Repmästaren** (knot 10). The combat part moved to `src/minigames/trassel/strid.ts`, shared by both modes. Tests: every rope knot 1–16 over 25 seeds is tangled and solvable in `par`, the spider's step is always undone by one move, the greedy hint solves ≥ 90 %.
- **Trasselvinden** (2026-10-18, a fourth mini-game on Spelön, asked for by the user, `#/trasselvinden`): an untangle (planarity) puzzle. Drag pegs until no ropes cross (red = crossing, a peg lying on a rope also counts). Each knot is a jittered grid with some diagonals and some ropes removed (always solvable), scrambled onto a circle; it grows from 4 pegs (knot 1) to 25 (knot 14+). Meanwhile attic monsters crawl in, few at first (`spawnEvery`: 20 s at knot 1 → 4 s at knot 11, `maxMons`): 🐀/🦇/🐍 walk to the hero and bite, 🕷️ (from knot 3) yanks a peg somewhere random (not one being held), 🧌 Knuttrollet on every 5th knot. Tap a monster to hit it.
  - Gear (`src/platform/rpg/trassel.ts`, `knutNumbers()`): Kraft = damage, Snabbhet = shorter wait between hits, Tur = crits + blad drops, Skydd = hp/armour, Klurighet = "Klurig knuff" hints (moves the worst peg to the best spot found on a grid), Visdom = hp back per solved knot. Weapons: yxa splash, stav chains, båge fast/weak, hammare stuns; sköld blocks, trollbok zaps all every 15 s, lykta slows monsters; the pet bites. Classes add +hp/+crit/+hint/+heal.
  - A run continues knot after knot until the child goes home or falls; all blad are kept either way. Start points every 5 knots (`knutStarts`). Keys: a chest on every 2nd solved knot (max 3). `HeroState.trassel` (best knot, runs; a new field with a default, no migration). Title **Knutmästaren** (knot 10).
  - Pure sim in `src/minigames/trassel/sim.ts`, SVG view in `View.tsx` (lazy chunk ~14 KB). Tests: `tests/trasselvinden.test.ts` (every knot 1–16 solvable and tangled over many seeds, difficulty rises, monsters/weapons/hints, a bot that clears 6 knots). **Needs a playtest** for balance (spawn rate, monster hp/damage, blad per knot).

## How to add a drill area

Tiokompisbron, Tabellgruvan, Myntmarknaden, Tallinjebron and Bråkbageriet are the templates; the Betongbukten parks (`src/games/geometri/<game>/`) show drawn SVG visuals (shared helpers in `geometri/draw.tsx`), the toolbox and answer tolerance.
1. `src/games/<game>/logic.ts`: seeded `makeQ(spec, rng)` returning a `DrillQ` (`parts` with exactly one `null` box – possibly inside a `Frac` –, `answer`, `skill`, optional `prompt`, `choices`, `keypad`, `tol` for rounded answers, `tools: 'kladd' | 'raknare'` for the toolbox on harder stages only), plus `specsForSkill`, `diagnose`, `explain` (never state the answer), `solution`, `hint` and `ERR` labels. Boss traps: `makeQ` must produce a question that can trigger `spec.trap`.
2. `index.tsx`: `drillModule({ id, make, specsForSkill, diagnose, explain, solution, hint, Visual?, notes?, errLabels })` (`notes(q)` = the formula card).
3. `content.ts`: an `AreaDef` with `texts`, eras, stages, monster bosses (trap + pet) and a final `mastare`; a generic persona in `src/content/bosses.ts` under `<area>.mastare`; the trap's pet stat in `PET_STAT` (`inventory.ts`); skill names in `src/content/skills.ts`.
4. Register in `src/content/areas.ts`, set `status: 'open'` in `world.ts`, and add the game to `GAMES` in `tests/drill-games.test.ts`. The generic tests then cover it (that file, and the playthrough in `tests/talbergen.test.ts`); put game-specific trap/misconception tests in their own file (like `tests/betongbukten.test.ts`).
5. A new region boss: an `AreaDef` with `region` (see `src/games/talbergen.ts`, `src/games/geometri/betongbukten.ts`), registered in `PLAYABLE` with `regionGame(id, gamesOf(id))`, plus a persona under `<region>.region`.
